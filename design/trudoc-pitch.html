The insight

The best medical-travel prospect is a patient someone is already managing.

A facilitator spends heavily to find a stranger with a heart condition, then works to earn their trust from zero. You start with both. You hold the relationship, the history, and — through your clinicians — the moment an elective procedure becomes the right call.

When that conversation happens, you are the trusted voice in the room. India, at a fraction of the cost and at accredited hospitals, is simply on the table.

Chronic care is a pipeline to surgical care. TruDoc sits at the top of it for millions of people. Today that signal goes nowhere. This is how it becomes a service and a margin.

The payer angle

Your insurers and TPAs will want this as a product.

The clients you already serve — insurers, TPAs, governments — carry the cost of these procedures. A managed India pathway is a line item they will actively want: accredited (JCI / NABH) hospitals, 60–90% lower cost, coordinated end to end.

Together we can offer it as a packaged benefit: lower claims cost for the payer, faster access for the member, and a new margin for TruDoc — sitting naturally alongside Vibrance Care and your chronic-management programmes. MedYatra is what makes it deliverable at scale.

What we plug into your operation

Five parts, one engine — white-labelled to TruDoc.

01 Lead ingestion

Your flagged members stream in through one secure endpoint. We normalise, mask the PII, dedupe, and place each into the funnel — tagged to you, isolated from everyone else.

02 Comms engine

A full patient-journey state machine on WhatsApp: first touch to booked patient, template- and 24-hour-session-aware, with a diagnosis fork and clean handoffs to the hospital's medical team. Nudge loops, opt-out and edge cases handled.

03 Multilingual content

Arabic-native and English guides that earn organic demand and answer the real questions — honest, specific, written to the patient. Cost infographics built from verified numbers.

04 Approval console

Nothing goes out until your team approves it. Every page, message and quote clears a human gate with automated QA — verified numbers, no vague claims, consent, regulatory clearance. Compliant by construction.

05 Supporting documents

We coordinate the hospital's invitation letter and the visa checklist. The patient applies for their own visa and books their own travel — coordination stays light, liability stays narrow.

✳ Wellness recovery

An optional naturopathy recovery stay — a calm, well-priced way to heal before flying home. It fits your care ethos and deepens the member relationship.

The part every operator asks about first

Your members' data stays yours. Full stop.

Strict tenant isolation. Your leads are siloed to TruDoc. No other operator can see them, and no member's record is ever reused for anyone else. This is enforced in the system, not merely promised — your team's view shows only your members.

What the platform learns is anonymous. Only de-identified, aggregate patterns — which categories and markets convert, how the funnel performs — inform the shared model, computed with a privacy threshold that suppresses any group small enough to identify. Aggregate learning, never data sharing.
