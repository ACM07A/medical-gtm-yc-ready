What we plug into your operation

Five parts, one engine — white-labelled to TruDoc.

01 Lead ingestion

Stream your patient or enquiry list in through one secure endpoint. We normalise it, mask the PII, dedupe, and put each lead into the funnel — tagged to you, isolated from everyone else.

02 WhatsApp comms engine

A full patient-journey state machine: first touch to booked patient, template- and 24h-session-aware, with a diagnosis fork and clean handoffs to the hospital's medical team. Nudge loops, opt-out, and edge cases handled.

03 Multilingual content

Arabic-native and English guides that earn organic demand — honest, specific, and written to the patient, not at them. Cost infographics built from real numbers.

04 The approval console

Nothing goes out until your team approves it. Every page, message, and quote passes a human gate with automated QA (verified numbers, no vague claims, consent, regulatory clearance) — compliant by construction.

05 Supporting documents

We coordinate the hospital's invitation letter and the visa checklist. The patient applies for their own visa and books their own travel — coordination stays light, liability stays narrow.

✳ Wellness recovery

An optional naturopathy recovery stay — a calm, well-priced way for patients to heal before flying home. India does this credibly; it deepens the relationship.

The part every operator asks about first

Your patients' data stays yours. Full stop.

Strict tenant isolation. Your leads are siloed to TruDoc. No other operator can see them, and no patient record is ever reused for anyone else. This is enforced in the system, not just promised — your team's view shows only your patients.

What the platform learns is anonymous. Only de-identified, aggregate patterns — which categories and markets convert, how the funnel performs — inform the shared model, computed with a privacy threshold that suppresses any group small enough to identify. That is aggregate learning, never patient-data sharing.
