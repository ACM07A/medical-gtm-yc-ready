FAQ

Is cancer treatment in India safe? We work with established Indian hospitals that maintain JCI or NABH accreditations and have dedicated international patient departments. We will share hospital details so you can review them.

How long will we need to stay? Length of stay depends on the treatment plan. For bone marrow transplants, expect several weeks in India for the procedure and initial recovery. Your treating doctor will advise you after assessment.

Do we pay you or the hospital? You pay the hospital directly for treatment. We do not handle your medical funds.
