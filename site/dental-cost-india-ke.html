Dental Treatment Cost in India for Patients from Kenya

In short: Travelling from Kenya to India for dental care can offer large savings, short waiting times, and access to internationally accredited facilities.

Why Patients from Kenya Choose India

  • Significant cost savings: Dental packages in India are typically a fraction of what you would pay in the UK or other popular medical travel destinations, without compromising on quality materials.
  • Accredited hospitals: Many leading Indian dental centres hold international recognitions such as JCI or national NABH accreditation, reflecting strong standards in patient safety and care.
  • Short waits: Appointment lead times are generally much shorter, meaning you can begin treatment sooner rather than waiting months for a local slot.
  • Familiar travel routes: Direct and connecting flights between Nairobi and major Indian cities make the journey relatively straightforward for Kenyan patients.

Frequently Asked Questions
