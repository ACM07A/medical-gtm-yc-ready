Canopus Care

Cross-border care coordination, from intake to return home.

A human-supervised operating system for medical travel facilitators, bringing cases, hospital responses, vendors, approvals, and audit history into one clear workflow.

One operational thread

A complicated journey, made legible.

Canopus Care keeps administrative coordination in sequence while preserving consent, role boundaries, and human control.
