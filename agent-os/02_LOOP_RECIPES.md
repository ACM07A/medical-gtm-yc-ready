# 02 — Loop Recipes

## Category refresh
/goal category scoring recomputed from current cited data; ranked top-6 + category↔market matrix updated; no UNVERIFIED inputs in the portfolio — stop after 4 turns

## Partner build (per category)
/goal ≥5 scored anchor hospitals for [category] with a verified public IPS/MVT POC each, written to CRM with source citations — stop after 6 turns

## Proposal batch
/goal tailored 8-section proposals drafted for [N hospitals], each human-approved before any send — stop after 5 turns

## Content engine (weekly, proactive)
/loop weekly identify (category×market×language) content gaps → draft top-10 assets → localize → route clinical claims to review → schedule/publish → ingest performance → feed back

## Feeder network
/goal ≥20 source-market referrers identified with public business contacts + outreach drafted (human-gated) — stop after 5 turns

## Lead funnel QA
/goal WhatsApp→qualification→CRM path works end-to-end for a test lead; SLA timers fire — stop after 3 turns

## New-market launch
/goal new source market goes live via config only: content grid, channel mix, compliance surface, and partner targets all derive from the config with zero core-agent code change — stop after 6 turns

## Compliance sweep
/goal no published asset has an uncited clinical/price claim, cure/outcome guarantee, or PII-in-model-prompt violation; all fixed — stop after 5 turns
