# Compliance

MedYatra is positioned as a facilitator workflow system, not a healthcare provider.

Hard boundaries:

- No diagnosis.
- No scan, lab or prescription interpretation.
- No treatment recommendation.
- No outcome promise.
- No fitness-to-fly declaration.
- No real patient records in demo mode.
- No live WhatsApp, email, social, vendor booking or payments unless explicitly configured and human-approved.

Demo clearances, estimates, vendors and patient data are illustrative. Clinical suitability is displayed as a hospital-owned reviewer status, never as an AI decision.

The seeded exception case `case_amina_okoro` intentionally blocks communication with `CONSENT_REQUIRED`.
