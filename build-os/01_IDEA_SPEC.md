# 01 — Idea Spec

## Product
An agent-run GTM engine that stands up and scales a medical-tourism facilitation business. It continuously optimizes **what to sell** (treatment portfolio), **who supplies it** (hospital + ecosystem partners), and **how demand is generated** (multilingual content-driven brand), and it re-points to any source/destination market via config.

## Core problem
Launching medical tourism means simultaneously solving a two-sided cold-start (need hospitals to attract patients, need patients to attract hospitals), at multilingual scale, in a trust-critical, compliance-heavy category. Doing this manually is slow and expensive. The winning competitors are essentially content + BD machines — which is exactly what an agent fleet can operate.

## Users
- **Founder/operator** (primary) — supervises agents, closes big deals, sets strategy.
- **Patient coordinators** — handle qualified leads and patient care.
- **International patients** (end demand) — searching for affordable, trustworthy treatment.
- **Hospital IPS teams & feeder referrers** (supply) — partners the engine recruits.

## MVP scope (lead corridor: India × Arabic Middle East + English Africa track, top-6 categories)
Category scoring live · 5+ scored hospitals/category with POCs · proposal generator · SEO+WhatsApp content loop in English + Arabic · lead qualification into CRM · compliance guardrails.

## v1.5 / v2
Add remaining Africa + SE Asia source markets · feeder network automation · paid-acquisition loop · European dental/cosmetic/fertility corridor · second destination country · analytics + attribution · self-serve partner portal.

## Monetization
Facilitation fee 20% entry (revenue-tiered to 16%, vs incumbents' 25–33%) from hospitals (primary) · ancillary (visa/travel/stay/interpreter) · feeder-network commission share · later: premium concierge tier.

## Risks
Trust/brand in a scam-prone category · compliance missteps · hospital commercial resistance · content quality vs. clinical accuracy · platform ad-policy bans on health · over-automation eroding the human empathy the category needs.
