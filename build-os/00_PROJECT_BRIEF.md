# 00 — Project Brief

## One-line

An AI-run go-to-market engine that launches and scales a medical-tourism facilitation business in India — deciding which treatments to sell, acquiring hospital partners, and running a content-driven brand campaign — built to be re-pointed at any source market globally.

## The business we are entering

Medical Value Travel (MVT) to India. India is a top-3 global destination: JCI/NABH-accredited hospitals delivering US/UK-equivalent clinical quality at **60–90% lower cost**. The government "Heal in India" initiative + e-Medical Visa reduce friction. ~2M+ international patients/year pre-pandemic, growing double digits.

**Our role:** facilitator / aggregator (like Vaidam, MediGence, Medtripz, Lyfboat). We connect international patients to accredited hospitals and earn a **facilitation fee / referral commission (20% entry rising to a 25% cap across three revenue tiers, always ≤ incumbents' 25–33%)** from the hospital, plus optional ancillary revenue (visa, travel, accommodation, interpreter, post-op). We are **not** a clinical provider.

## Why an *agentic* engine (not just a website + BD team)

Competitors win on two flywheels that are both labor-intensive: (1) SEO/content at massive multilingual scale, and (2) hospital BD + patient coordination. Both are automatable with an agent fleet:

- Content flywheel → a **Content Engine agent** produces multilingual, high-intent SEO + social at a cost/velocity a human team can't match.
- Supply flywheel → a **Partner Sourcing + Proposal agent** builds and maintains the hospital network and outreach.
- Category flywheel → a **Category Intelligence agent** keeps the treatment portfolio pointed at the highest cost-arbitrage × quality × demand cells.

The human team supervises, closes high-value deals, and handles the empathy-critical patient moments. Agents do the tireless research, drafting, scoring, and scheduling.

## Target source markets (India launch, priority order)

Focus regions: **Middle East · Africa · Central Asia · Europe · South East Asia** (Bangladesh explicitly excluded). Grounded in India's 2024 MVT arrivals — ex-Bangladesh, the largest sources are Iraq (~32k), Oman, Somalia, Nigeria, Yemen, Sudan, Kenya — and in the industry's active shift to *diversify away from Middle-East concentration* into Africa and SE Asia (a hedge against regional instability). Our tiering mirrors that reality:

- **Tier A — Middle East (largest MVT revenue):** Iraq, Oman, Yemen, plus Gulf value markets UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait. Arabic-first. This is where real deal value concentrates.
- **Tier B — Africa (diversification + volume, English-efficient content):** Nigeria, Kenya, Ethiopia, Sudan, Tanzania, Ghana.
- **Tier C — South East Asia (fast-growing diversification):** Myanmar, plus emerging Cambodia, Vietnam, Indonesia, Philippines (+ Indian-Ocean Maldives).
- **Tier D — Europe (high-margin electives):** UK & Ireland for dental, cosmetic, fertility, bariatric, and wellness — NHS-waitlist and price-driven.

**Lead proving corridor:** India × Arabic Middle East (Iraq + Oman/Gulf) — deepest real demand and revenue — run in parallel with an **English Africa track (Nigeria/Kenya)** for cheap, fast content velocity.

## Success metrics (first 12 months)

- 25+ signed accredited hospital partners across the 6 priority categories.
- Qualified-lead pipeline: 1,500+ MQLs/month by month 9, ≥ 8% MQL→treated conversion.
- Content: 500+ indexed SEO pages, top-10 ranking for 100+ high-intent "cost of X in India" queries, 3 source-market languages live.
- Blended CAC below competitor benchmark; positive contribution margin per treated patient.

## Explicitly NOT building (yet)

- Owning/operating clinics. Insurance underwriting. A telemedicine product of our own. A generic hospital-listing marketplace with no curation. Anything requiring us to give medical advice.
