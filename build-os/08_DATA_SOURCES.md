# 08 — Data Sources (evidence spine)

**Rule:** every price, outcome, accreditation, ranking, and demand claim in this engine must trace to a source listed here with a retrieval date. No source = mark `UNVERIFIED` = do not publish. This is a health business; fabrication is a stop-the-line event.

## Category / demand intelligence
- Google Keyword Planner / Search Console / Ahrefs–Semrush — search volume for "cost of X in India" per source-market geo.
- Medical Value Travel reports: FICCI, EY, NATHEALTH, Ministry of Tourism (India), "Heal in India" initiative.
- Competitor coverage maps: Vaidam, MediGence, Medtripz, Lyfboat, ClinicSpots (what categories/markets they push).

## Clinical quality / accreditation
- **JCI** accredited-organization directory. **NABH** accredited-hospital list (India).
- Hospital-published outcome data, procedure volumes, doctor credentials (verify on hospital + medical-council sources).
- India National Medical Commission / state council registries for doctor verification.

## Pricing
- Hospital international-patient package sheets (obtained via partnership).
- Source-market price references (local hospital/insurer benchmarks) for the comparison tables.
- Never invent a price. If only a range is sourced, publish the range with the citation.

## Partner / POC discovery (public data only)
- Hospital international-patient microsites & IPS department pages.
- LinkedIn (public profiles, title search) for IPS/MVT/International Business roles.
- MVT conference/summit speaker & exhibitor lists (FICCI Heal-in-India, Medical Fair India, etc.).
- Press releases, chamber-of-commerce directories, source-market clinic directories (feeder network).

## Compliance / regulatory
- India DPDP Act 2023; GDPR (EU source markets); platform health-ad policies (Meta, Google, YouTube); WhatsApp Business Policy; India e-Medical Visa rules; organ-transplant legal framework (THOTA).

## Evidence log
Each published claim → row in `/agent-os/12_EVIDENCE_LOG.md` (analog): `claim | value | source | url | retrieved_date | verified_by`.
