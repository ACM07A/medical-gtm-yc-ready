## B · Worked example — Apollo Heart Institutes × Iraq (cardiac)

**To:** Head, International Patient Services, Apollo Hospitals *(resolve named person via LinkedIn before send; public desk: newpatient@apollohospitalinternational.com / +91 80760 36335)*
**Re:** Cardiac (CABG / valve / angioplasty) partnership for Iraq & the Gulf

**1. Who we are.** MedYatra facilitates medical value travel to India, generating pre-qualified cardiac patients through Arabic + English high-intent content (SEO, YouTube, WhatsApp) across Iraq, Oman, and Yemen — currently India's largest non-Bangladesh MVT source region. Facilitator, not provider.

**2. Demand we bring.** Target 40–70 qualified cardiac leads/month from Iraq/Gulf by month 6, pre-screened for procedure (CABG vs. valve vs. angioplasty), clinical urgency, budget band, and report-readiness. You receive treatment-ready patients with reports in hand.

**3. Commercial model.** Facilitation fee **12%** of package value, billed to Apollo, never charged to the patient. Terms: net-15 post-discharge. Non-exclusive.

**4. Service-level asks.** Cardiac quote turnaround ≤ 48h · named coordinator for our patients · CABG/valve/angioplasty package sheet · pre-arrival cardiologist teleconsult · priority OT scheduling.

**5. Patient-experience split.** MedYatra: Arabic interpreter, visa-invitation coordination, airport pickup, accommodation, post-op tele-follow-up in Arabic. Apollo: clinical care, in-hospital coordinator, discharge summary + medication plan.

**6. Co-marketing.** Feature Apollo cardiac surgeons + consented Iraqi patient journeys in our Arabic cardiac content; Apollo listed on "CABG cost in India for patients from Iraq" landing page.

**7. Data & compliance.** Consent-based sharing only; DPDP (India) adherence; Arabic-language consent capture; no records shared without patient authorization.

**8. Pilot.** 90-day pilot, target 25 cardiac patients, success = ≥ 15 treated at ≤ 48h avg quote turnaround; review gate day 90.
